\section*{Part A: Multiple Choice (20 points)}

Choose the \textbf{best answer} for each question. Each question is worth 4 points.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
    \item Which of the following is the standard form of a quadratic function?
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
        \item $y = mx + b$
        \item $y = ax^2 + bx + c$
        \item $y = a(x - h)^2 + k$
        \item $y = \frac{1}{x}$
    \end{enumerate}

    \item If $a > 0$ in the quadratic function $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$, the parabola:
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
        \item Opens downward
        \item Opens upward
        \item Is a straight line
        \item Has no vertex
    \end{enumerate}

    \item What is the axis of symmetry of the function $f(x) = x^2 - 6x + 5$?
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
        \item $x = 6$
        \item $x = -6$
        \item $x = 3$
        \item $x = -3$
    \end{enumerate}

    \item How many real solutions does the equation $x^2 + 4x + 5 = 0$ have?
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
        \item Two real solutions
        \item One real solution
        \item No real solutions
        \item Infinitely many solutions
    \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Part C: Applications and Word Problems (30 points)}

Show all steps and explain your reasoning. Each question is worth 10 points.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
    \item A ball is thrown upward from a height of 2 meters. Its height after $t$ seconds is given by the function:
    \[
    h(t) = -5t^2 + 20t + 2
    \]
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
        \item What is the maximum height of the ball?
        \item At what time does the ball reach this maximum height?
    \end{enumerate}

    \vspace{4cm}

    \item A rectangular garden has a perimeter of 40 meters. Let the length be $x$ meters.
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
        \item Write a quadratic expression for the area of the garden.
        \item Find the maximum possible area.
    \end{enumerate}

    \vspace{4cm}

    \item A company models its profit (in dollars) using the function:
    \[
    P(x) = -2x^2 + 120x - 1000
    \]
    where $x$ is the number of products sold.
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
        \item How many products must be sold to achieve maximum profit?
        \item What is the maximum profit?
    \end{enumerate}

    \vspace{4cm}
\end{enumerate}
